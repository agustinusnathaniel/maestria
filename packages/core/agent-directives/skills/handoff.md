# Handoff Contract

For delegated work, keep these seven fields concise and evidence-based:

1. **Goal** - outcome and why
2. **Context** - relevant paths, constraints, decisions, and attempts
3. **Requirements** - expectations and boundaries
4. **Known problems** - risks and prior issues
5. **Assumptions documented** - tag uncertain assumptions `[inferred]` with evidence
6. **Success criteria** - observable completion promise
7. **Next step** - the next owner or action

Write `none` when a field does not apply. Report success, blocked, or failed; include changed files at signature/interface level, verification evidence, and blockers/follow-ups. Re-read the artifact before handoff.

End every delegated brief with: "If anything is unclear, exhaust available data, document your assumption, and proceed."
